# SQL Business Analysis — Northwind Database

## Business Problem
Without clear visibility into sales performance, businesses struggle 
to identify which products, customers, and markets drive the most 
revenue — and which are underperforming. This project analyses the 
Northwind database to answer seven key business questions across 
revenue trends, product performance, customer value, employee 
productivity, and geographic distribution, translating raw 
transactional data into actionable business insights.

## Dataset
- **Source:** [Northwind SQLite Database](https://github.com/jpwhite3/northwind-SQLite3/)
- **Period:** July 1996 – May 1998
- **Records:** 830 orders across 91 customers and 77 products
- **Region:** International — customers across 21 countries
- **Key Tables:** Orders, Order Details, Products, Customers, 
  Employees, Categories

## Methodology
1. Database exploration and schema mapping
2. SQL queries to answer seven business questions
3. Results exported to Pandas DataFrames for visualisation
4. Interactive Power BI dashboard
